# Why These E-Ink BSB Editions Were Created

The **Berean Standard Bible (BSB)** is a faithful, transparent, and readable modern English translation dedicated to the public domain. However, readers who download the standard `.epub` file from the official website onto dedicated e-readers often encounter frustrating limitations.

### Source & Version of the Text

* **Translation & Version:** **Berean Standard Bible (BSB), 3rd Printing** (complete canonical text).
* **Licensing:** Dedicated to the **Public Domain** (CC0 / Open Translation License).
* **Source Archive:** Compiled from the official **USFM 3.1** distribution (`bsb_usfm.zip`) from **Bible Hub** and **BereanBible.com** (not converted from an existing EPUB).
* **Upstream Release:** **October 2, 2026** (incorporating latest translation committee errata fixes).

### The Problem with the Standard EPUB

The standard digital EPUB online was built as a generic desktop export, not for specialized E-Ink hardware. On dedicated reading devices, it suffers from several major issues:

1. **Broken Table of Contents:** Chapter menus are flat or disorganized. On button-operated readers, reaching books like Romans or Revelation requires scrolling past more than 1,300 items.
2. **One-Way Footnotes:** Tapping a footnote link jumps to the end of the chapter with no return link back to your verse—leaving you stranded.
3. **Sluggish Performance & Crashes:** Complex internal structures and bloated chapter files cause laggy page turns and memory freezes on pocket e-readers.
4. **Distracting Typography:** Faint grays and uneven verse spacing cause line-height jitter on black-and-white screens.

### How These Editions Are Different

Rather than patching that flawed EPUB, this project compiled the entire Bible from scratch directly from official USFM source markup:

* **Fast Navigation:** Jump to any book or chapter in seconds—using physical buttons (via a streamlined 2-stage menu on the XTEINK X4) or touch gestures (via an expandable 3-level menu on Kobo/KOReader).
* **Effortless Footnotes:** Enjoy instant pop-up notes right over the text or dedicated end-of-book notes that always offer a direct return link back to your verse.
* **Tuned for E-Ink:** Crisp, pure-black text with uniform verse numbers, properly indented poetry, and clean section headings tailored specifically to monochrome displays.

Both editions are completely free, distraction-free, and crafted to keep your focus on Scripture.
